      Before your first class

      I’m not flexible at all. Can I come?

      Yes. Flexibility comes with practice, and you don’t need it to start. Begin with the beginners’ group or hatha.

      What should I bring?

      Comfortable clothes and a water bottle. We have mats, blocks and blankets at the studio. We practice barefoot.

      Is there a shower and changing room?

      Yes, two changing rooms and a shower. Bring a towel or ask at the front desk.

      Can I pause my pass?

      Yes, passes of 8 classes or more can be paused once, for up to 14 days.
